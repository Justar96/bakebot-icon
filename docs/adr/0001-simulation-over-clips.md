# Simulation over clips

The mascot is a living character, not a logo that plays a loop. Clips (CSS keyframes, Lottie, GIFs) cannot overlap a blink with a glance, cannot splat because they met a wall, and cannot be redirected by a new gaze intent. A continuous spring simulation can: one clock, interruptible springs, a travel region with contact, press, and impact.

Pose stays in CSS and life stays in the simulation; they write different elements so they compose. A pane state can therefore have a shape and still be alive. Callers direct the mascot with pane state, colour, and gaze intents — they do not sequence the motion.
